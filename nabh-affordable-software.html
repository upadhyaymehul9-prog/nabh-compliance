It helps hospitals and clinics organise their documentation, track compliance progress, and prepare systematically for NABH assessment. The assessment itself is conducted by NABH.

That is entirely your choice. Many hospitals use AccredReady as a fully self-service software and tool and manage their NABH preparation without a consultant.
