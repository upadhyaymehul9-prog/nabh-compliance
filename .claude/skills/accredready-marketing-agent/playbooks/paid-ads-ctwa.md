# Playbook: Click-to-WhatsApp Meta Ads (Phase 2)

Status: NOT ACTIVE. Do not plan or draft campaigns until the founder explicitly
confirms budget and channels 1-3 (WhatsApp outreach, content, consultants) have
produced at least 10 paying customers with known acquisition paths.

Why this channel (when ready): Click-to-WhatsApp ads (CTWA) consistently beat
landing-page forms for Indian healthcare buyers — published benchmarks show
roughly 40% lower cost per qualified lead, because Indian buyers respond faster
and drop off less in a WhatsApp chat than in a web form. Source: Ichelon
Consulting Group healthcare performance marketing India benchmarks (2026).

## Preconditions checklist

- [ ] Founder-approved monthly budget (benchmark entry point: ₹50,000/month)
- [ ] WhatsApp motion proven organically (playbook 1 running, replies handled within 2 hours)
- [ ] "How did you hear about us?" field live in signup (attribution)
- [ ] At least 2 commercial-intent pages live (for retargeting destinations)
- [ ] Meta Business Manager + WhatsApp Business set up

## Campaign structure (when activated)

Audience: hospital administrators, quality/QA job titles, owner-doctors;
geo-target states with active ELC incentive schemes first.

Hook formula: specific problem + fast payoff, straight into WhatsApp chat.
Examples to adapt:
- "Preparing for NABH assessment? See your gap analysis in 10 minutes."
- "Your quality team reconciles 6 spreadsheets before every audit. There's a simpler way."

First WhatsApp reply (within minutes, founder or trained responder):
qualify programme + timeline, then send the matching /learn resource and offer
the 10-minute walkthrough. Same conversation flow as outreach touches T4-T6.

## Budget discipline

- Start with one campaign, one audience, ₹1,500-2,000/day
- Measure cost per qualified conversation (programme + timeline known), not clicks
- Kill any ad set with no qualified conversation after ₹10,000 spend
- Weekly spend summary in the marketing report

## Compliance

- No fake urgency, no OE counts, no competitor names in ad copy
- All brand-skill voice rules apply to ad copy and chat replies
