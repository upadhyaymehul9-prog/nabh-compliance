# Playbook: NABH Consultant Partnerships

Why this channel: NABH consultants guide hospitals through accreditation, but
their standard scope excludes day-to-day implementation tracking — the hospital
must own that, and that is exactly what AccredReady does. A consultant serving
10-50 facilities is a distribution channel, not competition. One good
partnership can bring dozens of accounts at zero acquisition cost.

Positioning with consultants: AccredReady makes their engagements stickier and
their clients more likely to pass. We complement their advice with a tracking
layer; we never position as replacing consultants.

## The offer

Lead with value to the consultant, in this order:

1. **Free consultant dashboard** — track all their client facilities'
   readiness in one place (multi-facility view). They see live progress
   instead of chasing clients for spreadsheet updates.
2. **Referral arrangement** — commission or extended-free-months for client
   facilities they bring (founder sets exact terms per partner).
3. **Co-marketing** — quote them (with credentials) in /learn articles and
   LinkedIn posts; assessor/consultant bylines are high-value GEO signals per
   `geo-signals.csv`.

## Finding consultant targets

- LinkedIn: "NABH consultant", "hospital accreditation consultant",
  "healthcare quality consultant" (India)
- Firms visible in NABH-related search results and directories
- Assessors and empanelled trainers who also consult
- Authors of NABH content on LinkedIn with real engagement

Qualify: number of active client facilities, programmes they serve, whether
they already push any software. Log in `data/consultants.csv`.

## The sequence

| Touch | Day | Channel | Content |
|-------|-----|---------|---------|
| P1 | 0 | LinkedIn / email | Peer-to-peer intro from Dr. Mehul: shared NABH work, one specific observation about their content or firm |
| P2 | 4 | Same | The problem framing: clients tracking readiness in scattered Excel between consultant visits; offer a 15-minute look at the tracking layer |
| P3 | 10 | WhatsApp / call | Demo call: show multi-facility view, agree pilot with 1-2 of their clients |
| P4 | after pilot | Call | Convert to `active`: referral terms, co-marketing plan |

P1 template (adapt):
> Hi [Name], I'm Mehul — I head medical services at HMP Foundation and built
> AccredReady, a NABH readiness tracker used by quality teams. Your work with
> [firm/facility] on [programme] caught my attention. Consultants tell us the
> hardest part of an engagement is what happens between their visits — clients
> lose track in spreadsheets. We built a consultant view for exactly that, and
> I'd value your read on it. Open to a 15-minute look?

## Partner success loop (for `active` partners)

- Monthly check-in: their clients' progress, blockers, feature asks
- Feature their expertise in one content piece per quarter (with byline)
- Ask for one introduction per month — specific, not "anyone you know"

## Cadence

- 3 new consultant targets identified and P1-touched per week
- All due follow-ups drafted in `/marketing weekly`
- Monthly check-in drafts for every `active` partner
