Workflow

Three steps from gap to survey-ready.

AccredReady maps the entire accreditation journey into a clear, repeatable workflow — so no standard gets missed.

1

Score every standard

Work chapter by chapter. Rate each objective element 1–5 with guidance built in. Your live readiness score updates in real time.

2

Identify & prioritise gaps

The platform ranks your gaps by severity, generates a chapter-wise compliance report, and tells you exactly what to fix first.

3

Fix, document & close

Create CAPA actions per gap, assign owners, attach evidence, and schedule committees and drills. Generate a PDF report when ready.
