## CapCut steps (phone or desktop)

1. **New project** → import `accredready-promo-1080p.mp4` (horizontal) or `accredready-promo-vertical.mp4` (Reels).
2. **Text → Auto captions** (optional) OR **Text → Import → SRT** → select `accredready-promo.srt` to show lines on screen while you record.
3. **Audio → Record** (or record on your phone voice memos first, then import):
   - Play the video and read each line when the matching scene appears.
   - Use the SRT lines as a teleprompter on a second device if easier.
4. **Audio → Music** → add a soft background track at **10–15% volume** (search "corporate calm" or "inspiring minimal" in CapCut's free library).
5. **Export** → 1080p, 30fps.
