# AccredReady Marketing Strategy

Owner: Dr. Mehul Upadhyay
Operated by: `accredready-marketing-agent` skill (`.claude/skills/accredready-marketing-agent/`)
Product: accredready.in — NABH compliance platform for Indian hospitals (HCO, SHCO, ELC, ECO programmes), ₹499/month, 14-day free trial.

## Positioning

AccredReady is built by a practising hospital quality manager for quality managers.
It replaces the scattered Excel sheets, WhatsApp forwards, and paper checklists that
hospitals currently use to prepare for NABH assessment. We position against the
problem (fragmented readiness tracking), never against other products.

## Who we sell to

| Persona | Facility | Why they buy |
|---------|----------|--------------|
| Quality manager / NABH coordinator | 50+ bed hospitals (HCO) | Owns assessment readiness, drowning in documentation |
| Administrator / owner-doctor | SHCOs, nursing homes | Needs ELC or SHCO accreditation for empanelment and incentives |
| Medical director / CMO | Multi-specialty hospitals | Accountable for accreditation outcome and renewals |
| NABH consultant | Serves 10-50 client facilities | Needs a tracking layer for clients; consultant scope excludes day-to-day implementation tracking |

## The five channels, in priority order

Chosen from documented results for bootstrapped Indian B2B SaaS. Full research
citations live in each playbook.

### 1. Founder-led WhatsApp outreach (start now, zero cost)

Structured personal WhatsApp outreach is the cheapest proven growth motion for
Indian B2B SaaS: reply rates near 68% vs 4-9% for cold email, CAC under ₹1,000.
Prospect on LinkedIn, connect, move the conversation to WhatsApp within a week,
lead with a specific NABH fact or free resource — never a pitch.
Playbook: `playbooks/whatsapp-outreach.md`. Tracker: `data/prospects.csv`.

### 2. Bottom-funnel SEO + AEO/GEO (extend what is built)

40+ SEO pages and the /learn hub already exist. The gap is commercial-intent
pages (comparison and decision pages), which convert evaluators rather than
readers. Every informational page must link to one commercial page. AI search
(ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews) is now a primary research channel for
healthcare buyers in India, so every page follows the AEO rules in the
`accredready-marketing` skill: answer-first opening, FAQ schema, named author,
citation to nabh.co or official NABH PDF.
Playbook: `playbooks/content-engine.md`. Backlog: `data/content-backlog.csv`.

### 3. NABH consultant partnerships (start now, zero cost)

Consultants guide hospitals through accreditation but their scope excludes
implementation tracking — exactly what AccredReady does. One consultant
partnership can bring dozens of facility accounts. Offer: free multi-client
dashboard and/or referral commission.
Playbook: `playbooks/consultant-partnerships.md`. Tracker: `data/consultants.csv`.

### 4. LinkedIn content engine (running, keep cadence)

Daily rotating posts from `marketing/linkedin/content-queue.json` (9 AM IST).
Keep the queue at 30+ posts, refreshed monthly, mixing tips, /learn links, and
video. LinkedIn is the prospecting surface that feeds channel 1.
Playbook: `playbooks/linkedin-engine.md`.

### 5. Click-to-WhatsApp Meta ads (phase 2, paid)

CTWA ads consistently beat landing-page forms for Indian healthcare buyers
(roughly 40% lower cost per qualified lead in published benchmarks). Do not
start until channels 1-3 prove the messaging and the WhatsApp motion exists to
receive conversations. Entry budget benchmark: ₹50,000/month.
Playbook: `playbooks/paid-ads-ctwa.md`.

## Measurement

- Single source of truth: the CSV trackers in the agent's `data/` folder plus
  Supabase signup data.
- Ask "How did you hear about us?" at signup (backlog item, app change) —
  captures WhatsApp forwards and consultant referrals that analytics miss.
- Weekly report generated by `scripts/marketing_status.py` + the reporting
  playbook: pipeline by stage, content shipped, queue health, next actions.
Playbook: `playbooks/measurement.md`.
