ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT

Delete Your Account

Two ways to permanently remove your AccredReady account and all associated data.

Before You Delete

⚠️ Deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once confirmed, there is no recovery option. We recommend exporting a PDF report of your compliance data before proceeding — use the ⬇ Export PDF button on the Dashboard screen.

If you only want to sign out of a device, use Profile → Sign out instead. Your data will remain intact and accessible when you sign in again.

What Gets Deleted

The following data is permanently and immediately removed from our database:

  • Your hospital profile and name
  • All assessments linked to your hospital
  • All OE scores and evidence links
  • All CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Action) records
  • All KPI data entries and custom KPI targets
  • All internal audit records and custom audit templates
  • All mock drill records
  • All committee meeting logs
  • All committee calendar plans
  • All checklist progress records
  • All statutory license records
  • All patient tracer records
  • Your authentication account (email address and password)

Any residual data in Supabase's automated backup snapshots is purged within 30 days as those backup windows expire. No manual retention occurs after that point.

Method 1 — In-App (Instant)

RECOMMENDED Available to all signed-in users
1
Open the app and sign in
Go to accredready.in and sign in with your email and password.
2
Navigate to Profile
In the top navigation bar, click ••• (More menu), then select 👤 Profile.
3
Scroll to Danger Zone
Scroll to the bottom of the Profile page to find the ⚠️ DANGER ZONE section.
4
Click "Delete Account"
Click the Delete Account button. A confirmation modal will appear describing everything that will be deleted.
5
Confirm deletion
Click Yes, delete everything to confirm. All data is deleted immediately and you are signed out. The process is complete — no further steps needed.

Method 2 — Email Request

ALTERNATIVE Use if you cannot access the app

If you are unable to sign in to the app, send an email to us and we will delete your account manually within 7 business days.

Send your request to:

dr.mehul_ku@yahoo.com

Use subject line: Account Deletion Request

Include the email address registered with your AccredReady account. We may ask for a brief verification to confirm account ownership before proceeding.

After Deletion

Once deleted, your email address is free to be used for a new AccredReady account at any time. Previous data will not be restored — a new account starts completely fresh.

If you have questions about your data or this process, review our Privacy Policy or contact us at dr.mehul_ku@yahoo.com.
