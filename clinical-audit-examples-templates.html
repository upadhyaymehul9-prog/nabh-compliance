Clinical Audit Examples
& Report Templates

The fastest way to understand clinical audit isn't another definition — it's seeing one done properly, start to finish. AccredReady's Examples library walks through four realistic hospital audits, each with the plain-English story and the full formal report structure.

Why worked examples beat a blank template

A blank clinical audit report template tells you what headings to fill in. It doesn't tell you what a realistic sample size looks like, how to phrase a measurable standard, what an "acceptable exception" actually is, or how to write a conclusion that's honestly supported by the data rather than inflated. That's the gap worked examples close.

The four examples inside AccredReady

1. Fall-Risk Assessment Completed Within 2 Hours of Admission

A full cycle, start to finish — topic selection through to the standard, sampling method, findings, and action plan for a time-bound process standard.

2. Antibiotic Given Within 60 Minutes Before Surgery

Shows how to turn a clinical standard into a real, measurable result — including how to handle a fair clinical exception without it becoming a loophole.

3. Consent Form Fully Completed Before a Procedure

Demonstrates finding the real pattern behind a compliance number, not just reporting one headline percentage.

4. Hand Hygiene Compliance Before Touching a Patient

A complete re-audit example — the evidence an assessor (and your own CQI committee) actually wants: proof a fix worked, not just a promise it should.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these real hospital audits?

No — the examples use realistic but fictional topics and numbers, built to teach the method clearly without reproducing any real patient or hospital data.

Can I download the report templates?

Yes, each worked example includes a downloadable report mock-up showing the full formal structure, available once you sign in.
