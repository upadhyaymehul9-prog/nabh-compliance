## Statement of intent

Those responsible for governance manage the organisation in an ethical manner.

## 1. Purpose

This policy says how Preview Hospital meets NABH Hospitals 6th Edition standard ROM.2: Those responsible for governance manage the organisation in an ethical manner.

It covers objective elements ROM.2.a–d (4 elements).

Chapter intent (official Standards PDF): The management of the healthcare organisation is aware of and manages all the key components of governance. Those responsible for governance are identified and their roles defined. The standards encourage the governance of the organisation professionally and ethically. Clinical governance framework is established, that includes clinical audits, clinical pathways, education and research. The responsibilities of management are defined. The responsibilities of the leaders at all levels are defined. The management executes its responsibility for compliance with all applicable regulations. Those responsible for governance address the organisations social responsibility. Leaders ensure that patient-safety and risk-management issues are an integral part of patient care and hospital management. The Organisation has a written guidance in place for change management and service continuity plan.

This policy owns ROM.2. Related AAC, COP, MOM, PRE, IPC and PSQ duties stay with those policies — cross-reference only. Other ROM standards stay with their own policies.

Words marked «like this» are defaults. A blank marked «________» must be filled before issue.

## 5. What we do

### 5.1 The leaders establish the organisation’s ethical management framework

Leaders establish a written ethical-management framework: principles, who decides, how issues are raised, and how decisions are recorded.

The «Medical Superintendent» keeps the current framework. Minutes-only notes are not this CORE asterisked element.

Method note (from guidebook interpretation): The organisation shall function ethically. Transparency in its actions shall be one of its guiding principles. Handling of complaints, grievances, clinical care delivery and research shall be some of the areas to address. The framework includes codes of conduct. A good reference guide for minimum code of conduct for doctors is "Code of medical ethics, 2002" published by Medical Council of India.

This objective element is asterisked in the official Standards PDF — documentation of the process is required.

This is a CORE objective element — non-compliance is not acceptable for accreditation.

### 5.2 The ethical management framework includes processes for managing issu...

The framework includes processes for managing issues with ethical implications, dilemmas and concerns (clinical and organisational examples the organisation names).

A named path, timeline and record exist. An informal corridor discussion is not this asterisked element.

Method note (from guidebook interpretation): The issues may be clinical or non-clinical. Some examples include conflict of interest, breach of confidentiality, ethical behaviour, informed consent, and withdrawing life-sustaining treatment. There shall be a consistent approach to ethical decision making throughout the organisation based on the guiding principle(s). Staff shall be provided with appropriate guidance with respect to their actions based on these principles. The approach shall include process for collecting, collating and analysing ethical dilemmas for taking corrective and preventive actions within a defined time frame. Ethical dilemma could arise when there are conflicting decisions regarding provision or withdrawal of life support or treatment. Decisions made regarding ethical issues shall be documented, and opportunities for improvement could be identified.

This objective element is asterisked in the official Standards PDF — documentation of the process is required.

### 5.3 The organisation discloses its ownership

The organisation discloses its ownership in a defined public place (reception display, website, statutory board as applicable).

The «Medical Superintendent» holds the current disclosure text and checks it «annually».

Method note (from guidebook interpretation): Hospitals are governed under the provisions of Companies Act, Charitable Trust Act, Societies Registration Act, Limited Liability Partnership Act, sole proprietorship and Government of India/State Government. The ownership of the hospital shall be disclosed accordingly. The disclosure could be in the form of a registration certificate.

### 5.4 The organisation honestly portrays its affiliations and accreditations

The organisation honestly portrays its affiliations and accreditations: only current, in-scope programmes are displayed; expired or applied-for status is not shown as awarded.

The «Quality Coordinator» reviews public materials «quarterly».

Method note (from guidebook interpretation): It implies that the organisation conveys its affiliations, accreditations for specific departments or whole hospital in an honest manner, wherever such exist.
