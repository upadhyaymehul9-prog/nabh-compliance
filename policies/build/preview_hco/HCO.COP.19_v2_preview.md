## Statement of intent

Nutritional therapy is provided to patients consistently and collaboratively.

## 1. Purpose

This policy says how Preview Hospital meets NABH Hospitals 6th Edition standard COP.19: Nutritional therapy is provided to patients consistently and collaboratively.

It covers objective elements COP.19.a–e (5 elements).

This policy owns COP.19. Related AAC, PRE, IPC/HIC, HRM and MOM duties stay with those policies — cross-reference only.

Words marked «like this» are defaults. A blank marked «________» must be filled before issue.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to staff who deliver or oversee the care described in COP.19 at Preview Hospital, including the «Dietetics In-Charge», treating doctors, nurses and the Quality Coordinator.

It covers 5 objective elements (COP.19.a, COP.19.b, COP.19.c, COP.19.d, COP.19.e).

Boundaries: do not copy SHCO COP wording. Do not overwrite AAC policies. Spell out abbreviations on first use in training materials.

## 3. Policy standards

Preview Hospital implements COP.19 so that nutritional therapy is provided to patients consistently and collaboratively..

Staff follow written guidance, keep the records listed in the OE table, and escalate when stop-work triggers fire (if this policy includes a stop-work section).

## 4. Non-negotiable rules

1. Do not skip: Patients admitted to the organisation are screened for nutritional risk.
2. Do not skip: Nutritional assessment is done for patients found at risk during nutritional screening.
3. Do not skip: The therapeutic diet is planned and provided collaboratively.
4. Do not skip: Patients receive food according to the written order for the diet.
5. Do not skip: When families provide food, they are educated about the patient’s diet limitations.
6. Staff who see a COP.19 rule broken report it the same shift to the «department in-charge» or the «Medical Superintendent».
