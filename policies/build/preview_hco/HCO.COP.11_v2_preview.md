## Statement of intent

Organisation provides safe paediatric services.

## 1. Purpose

This policy says how Preview Hospital meets NABH Hospitals 6th Edition standard COP.11: Organisation provides safe paediatric services.

It covers objective elements COP.11.a–h (8 elements).

This policy owns COP.11. Related AAC, PRE, IPC/HIC, HRM and MOM duties stay with those policies — cross-reference only.

Words marked «like this» are defaults. A blank marked «________» must be filled before issue.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to staff who deliver or oversee the care described in COP.11 at Preview Hospital, including the «Paediatric In-Charge», treating doctors, nurses and the Quality Coordinator.

It covers 8 objective elements (COP.11.a, COP.11.b, COP.11.c, COP.11.d, COP.11.e, COP.11.f, COP.11.g, COP.11.h).

Boundaries: do not copy SHCO COP wording. Do not overwrite AAC policies. Spell out abbreviations on first use in training materials.

## 3. Policy standards

Preview Hospital implements COP.11 so that organisation provides safe paediatric services..

Staff follow written guidance, keep the records listed in the OE table, and escalate when stop-work triggers fire (if this policy includes a stop-work section).

## 4. Non-negotiable rules

1. Do not skip: Paediatric services are organised and provided safely.
2. Do not skip: Neonatal care is in consonance with the national / international guidelines.
3. Do not skip: Those who care for children have age-specific competency.
4. Do not skip: Provisions are made for special care of children.
5. Do not skip: Paediatric assessment includes growth, developmental, immunisation and nutritional assessment.
6. Do not skip: The organisation has measures in place to prevent child / neonate abduction and abuse.
7. Do not skip: The child’s family members are educated about nutrition, immunisation and safe parenting.
8. Do not skip: The organisation provides for adolescent friendly health care services.
9. Staff who see a COP.11 rule broken report it the same shift to the «department in-charge» or the «Medical Superintendent».

## 7. Quality monitoring (RCA → CAPA)

The Quality Coordinator audits this policy «quarterly».

What is monitored each quarter:

- Sample of records for each OE COP.11.a–h.
- Asterisked elements (abf) have document evidence as required.
- CORE elements (none) show no critical gaps in the sample.
- Stop-work events (if any) are logged with outcome.

This policy is reviewed «annually», and sooner after a related adverse event.
