## Statement of intent

Rehabilitation services are provided to the patients in a safe, collaborative and consistent manner.

## 1. Purpose

This policy says how Preview Hospital meets NABH Hospitals 6th Edition standard COP.18: Rehabilitation services are provided to the patients in a safe, collaborative and consistent manner.

It covers objective elements COP.18.a–g (7 elements).

This policy owns COP.18. Related AAC, PRE, IPC/HIC, HRM and MOM duties stay with those policies — cross-reference only.

Words marked «like this» are defaults. A blank marked «________» must be filled before issue.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to staff who deliver or oversee the care described in COP.18 at Preview Hospital, including the «Rehabilitation In-Charge», treating doctors, nurses and the Quality Coordinator.

It covers 7 objective elements (COP.18.a, COP.18.b, COP.18.c, COP.18.d, COP.18.e, COP.18.f, COP.18.g).

Boundaries: do not copy SHCO COP wording. Do not overwrite AAC policies. Spell out abbreviations on first use in training materials.

## 3. Policy standards

Preview Hospital implements COP.18 so that rehabilitation services are provided to the patients in a safe, collaborative and consistent manner..

Staff follow written guidance, keep the records listed in the OE table, and escalate when stop-work triggers fire (if this policy includes a stop-work section).

## 7. Quality monitoring (RCA → CAPA)

The Quality Coordinator audits this policy «quarterly».

What is monitored each quarter:

- Sample of records for each OE COP.18.a–g.
- Asterisked elements (none) have document evidence as required.
- CORE elements (none) show no critical gaps in the sample.
- Stop-work events (if any) are logged with outcome.

This policy is reviewed «annually», and sooner after a related adverse event.
