## Statement of intent

This policy says how Preview Hospital meets NABH Hospitals 6th Edition standard COP.5: Cardio-pulmonary resuscitation services are provided uniformly across the organisation.

## 6. Stop-work authority

Do not start a planned resuscitation response (or mock drill counted as CPR competence evidence) without a named CPR team with clear roles and without the minimum emergency medications and equipment available at the location.

Stop-work does not block an unexpected cardiac arrest already in progress — start CPR with available staff and escalate for equipment/team immediately.

The person responsible tells the «CPR Committee chair / Emergency In-Charge» the same shift. Refusing to run a hollow CPR response is not a disciplinary matter.
