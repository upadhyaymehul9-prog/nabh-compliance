## Statement of intent

End-of-life care is provided in a compassionate and considerate manner.

## 1. Purpose

This policy says how Preview Hospital meets NABH Hospitals 6th Edition standard COP.20: End-of-life care is provided in a compassionate and considerate manner.

It covers objective elements COP.20.a–e (5 elements).

This policy owns COP.20. Related AAC, PRE, IPC/HIC, HRM and MOM duties stay with those policies — cross-reference only.

Words marked «like this» are defaults. A blank marked «________» must be filled before issue.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to staff who deliver or oversee the care described in COP.20 at Preview Hospital, including the «Palliative / End-of-Life Care Lead», treating doctors, nurses and the Quality Coordinator.

It covers 5 objective elements (COP.20.a, COP.20.b, COP.20.c, COP.20.d, COP.20.e).

Boundaries: do not copy SHCO COP wording. Do not overwrite AAC policies. Spell out abbreviations on first use in training materials.

## 3. Policy standards

Preview Hospital implements COP.20 so that end-of-life care is provided in a compassionate and considerate manner..

Staff follow written guidance, keep the records listed in the OE table, and escalate when stop-work triggers fire (if this policy includes a stop-work section).

## 4. Non-negotiable rules

1. Do not skip: End-of-life care is provided in a consistent manner in the organisation.
2. Do not skip: A multi-professional approach is used to provide end-of-life care.
3. Do not skip: End-of-life care is in consonance with the legal requirements.
4. Do not skip: End-of-life care also addresses the identication of the unique needs of such patient and family.

## 6. Governance and responsibility

Medical Superintendent
- Accountable that COP.20 is resourced and followed.

Palliative / End-of-Life Care Lead
- Owns day-to-day implementation and records for this standard.

Treating doctors / nurses / technicians (as applicable)
- Deliver care as written; escalate stop-work triggers without delay.

Quality Coordinator
- Audits this policy «quarterly»; holds training acknowledgements.

## 7. Quality monitoring (RCA → CAPA)

The Quality Coordinator audits this policy «quarterly».

What is monitored each quarter:

- Sample of records for each OE COP.20.a–e.
- Asterisked elements (a) have document evidence as required.
- CORE elements (none) show no critical gaps in the sample.
- Stop-work events (if any) are logged with outcome.

This policy is reviewed «annually», and sooner after a related adverse event.
