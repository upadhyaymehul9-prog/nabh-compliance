## Statement of intent

Anaesthesia services are provided in a consistent and safe manner.

## 1. Purpose

This policy says how Preview Hospital meets NABH Hospitals 6th Edition standard COP.13: Anaesthesia services are provided in a consistent and safe manner.

It covers objective elements COP.13.a–j (10 elements).

This policy owns COP.13. Related AAC, PRE, IPC/HIC, HRM and MOM duties stay with those policies — cross-reference only.

Words marked «like this» are defaults. A blank marked «________» must be filled before issue.

This policy applies to staff who deliver or oversee the care described in COP.13 at Preview Hospital, including the «Anaesthesia In-Charge», treating doctors, nurses and the Quality Coordinator.

## 6. Stop-work authority

Do not induce anaesthesia when the pre-anaesthesia assessment and anaesthesia plan are not documented, when pre-induction assessment is not documented, or when required intra-operative monitoring (including ETCO2 where applicable) cannot be provided.

Stop-work applies to induction. Immediate life-saving operative care follows emergency anaesthesia rules documented by the organisation.

The person who stops tells the «Anaesthesiologist / OT In-Charge» the same shift. Refusing unsafe induction is not a disciplinary matter.
