## Statement of intent

Procedural sedation is provided consistently and safely.

## 1. Purpose

This policy says how Preview Hospital meets NABH Hospitals 6th Edition standard COP.12: Procedural sedation is provided consistently and safely.

It covers objective elements COP.12.a–h (8 elements).

This policy owns COP.12. Related AAC, PRE, IPC/HIC, HRM and MOM duties stay with those policies — cross-reference only.

Words marked «like this» are defaults. A blank marked «________» must be filled before issue.

This policy applies to staff who deliver or oversee the care described in COP.12 at Preview Hospital, including the «Anaesthesia / Sedation In-Charge», treating doctors, nurses and the Quality Coordinator.

## 6. Stop-work authority

Do not begin procedural sedation when pre-sedation assessment is incomplete, required monitoring is unavailable, or recovery/discharge criteria are not defined for the location.

Stop-work applies to sedation start, not to airway rescue already in progress.

The person who stops tells the «sedation privileged doctor» and the «Medical Superintendent» the same shift. Refusing unsafe sedation is not a disciplinary matter.
