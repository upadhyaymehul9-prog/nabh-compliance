The organisation protects and promotes patient and family rights and informs them

PRE.1.a
Level: Commitment | Star ★ (documentation required)

Verbatim OE text:
Patient and family rights and responsibilities are documented, displayed, and they
are made aware of the same.

Guidebook interpretation:
Organisation shall document the patient's rights and inform them of their
responsibilities. These shall be documented in consonance with the Charter of
Patients' Rights laid down by the statutory body. The rights and responsibilities
of the patients shall be displayed in the hospital where it is prominently visible
to patients, families and visitors. Pamphlets could be used to make them aware.
Information, education and communication material shall at least be bilingual.

Stop-work check: Commitment — no standalone stop-work trigger at this OE. PRE.1 has
no stop-work section. Failure to document, display or create awareness is a CAPA gap,
not an immediate stop-work trigger.

Mandatory (shall):
  1. Patient rights and responsibilities shall be documented in consonance with the
     Charter of Patients' Rights laid down by the statutory body.
  2. Rights and responsibilities shall be displayed where prominently visible to
     patients, families and visitors.
  3. Information, education and communication (IEC) material shall be at least bilingual.

Illustrative only:
  — "Pamphlets could be used to make them aware" — aspirational mechanism; the
    mandatory requirement is awareness, not pamphlets specifically.

Documentation: OE is starred — the documented set, display boards, and awareness records
(with date, language and named staff) must be on file.

PRE.1.d
Level: CORE | No star

Verbatim OE text:
The organisation has a mechanism to report a violation of patient and family rights.

Guidebook interpretation:
The organisation may develop a list of such instances which could be considered as
infringements of patients' and families' rights and train the staff accordingly. For
example, compromising the privacy, breaching confidentiality, disrespect to the
religious and cultural needs, not providing medical records within the stipulated time
etc. Violation of patient and family rights is reported through an incident reporting
form. It shall provide details of how the right was violated and where applicable by
whom. Also, there shall be a mechanism for the patient and/or family to report a
violation of their rights. The patient feedback form (by incorporating patient rights
worded appropriately) could be used as a tool to capture violation of patient rights.

Stop-work check: CORE — no standalone stop-work trigger at this OE. This OE is the
reporting mechanism for rights violations; violations themselves are reported here, they
are not individually stop-work triggers under PRE.1. (PRE.2 has its own stop-work for
abuse/privacy/consent violations in progress.)

Mandatory (shall):
  1. There shall be a mechanism for the patient and/or family to report a violation of
     their rights.
  2. The violation report shall provide details of how the right was violated and,
     where applicable, by whom.
  3. Violations of patient and family rights shall be reported through an incident
     reporting form.

Illustrative only:
  — "The organisation may develop a list of such instances" — aspirational; the list
    itself is not mandatory by this OE, but training staff on what constitutes an
    infringement is good practice.
  — Examples given (privacy compromise, confidentiality breach, disrespect to religious/
    cultural needs, records not provided in time) — illustrative list, not exhaustive.
  — "The patient feedback form... could be used as a tool" — aspirational mechanism;
    the mandatory requirement is the mechanism, not the specific tool.

CORE status: Absence of any mechanism for patients or families to report a rights
violation is a CORE gap. A mechanism that routes all reports only to the alleged
violator is not a valid mechanism.

Documentation: OE is not starred — the written mechanism (complaint box, helpline,
form), violation log with entries, and records of routes used by patients/families
are the expected supporting evidence.

PRE.1.e
Level: CORE | No star

Verbatim OE text:
Violation of patient and family rights are monitored, analysed, and corrective /
preventive action taken by the top leadership of the organisation.

Guidebook interpretation:
Whenever patients' rights have been infringed upon, management shall document the
said violations, investigate and maintain the record of the incident and its outcomes.
Corrective and/or preventive actions shall be taken in a time frame defined by the
organisation.

Stop-work check: CORE — no standalone stop-work trigger at this OE. This OE is the
CAPA and leadership oversight gate for violations already reported through PRE.1.d.

Mandatory (shall):
  1. Management shall document violations of patient and family rights.
  2. Management shall investigate violations and maintain the record of the incident
     and its outcomes.
  3. Corrective and/or preventive action shall be taken in a time frame defined by the
     organisation.
  4. Action shall be taken by the top leadership of the organisation (not delegated to
     line staff alone).

Illustrative only:
  — The specific time frame is for the organisation to define (the Guidebook does not
    prescribe a fixed number of days).

CORE status: Absence of documented investigation, absence of CAPA, or delegating
leadership review away from top leadership is a CORE gap. A dormant violation file
with no investigation is a CORE gap.

Documentation: OE is not starred — dated leadership review minutes, CAPA records with
owners and closure evidence, and the violation log are the expected supporting evidence.

PRE.2.b
Level: Commitment | No star

Verbatim OE text:
Patient and family rights include respect for personal dignity and privacy during
examination, procedures and treatment.

Guidebook interpretation:
During all stages of patient care, be it in the examination or carrying out a
procedure, hospital staff shall ensure that the patient's privacy and dignity are
maintained. The organisation shall develop the necessary guidelines for the same.
During procedures, the organisation shall ensure that the patient is exposed just
before the actual procedure. With regards to photographs/recording procedures, the
organisation shall ensure that explicit informed consent is taken and that the
patient's identity is not revealed.

[Note: ".." at end of source is a double-period OCR artefact; discarded.]

Stop-work check: Commitment — STOP-WORK TRIGGER: an examination or procedure that
exposes the patient beyond what the procedure requires, or that leaves the patient
without privacy, is a PRE.2 stop-work trigger (section 6). The stop-work applies to
the ongoing examination or procedure start.

Mandatory (shall):
  1. Hospital staff shall ensure that the patient's privacy and dignity are maintained
     during all stages of care — examination, procedures and treatment.
  2. The organisation shall develop written guidelines for maintaining privacy and dignity.
  3. The patient shall be exposed just before the actual procedure (not before).
  4. Explicit informed consent shall be taken for photographs or recordings of procedures.
  5. The patient's identity shall not be revealed in any photograph or recording.

Illustrative only:
  (none — all elements above are "shall")

Documentation: OE is not starred — privacy guidelines, any consent forms for
photography, and audit/observation records of privacy practice are the expected evidence.

Verbatim OE text:
Patient and family rights include protection from neglect or abuse.

patient protection records, and stop-work event logs (if triggered) are the expected
evidence.
