PSQ.5 — Raw Source Dump
=======================

Standard text (verbatim):
There is an established system for clinical audit.

OE count: 6 | COREs: none | Stars: none


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PSQ.5.a
Level: Commitment | No star

Verbatim OE text:
Clinical audits are performed to improve the quality of patient care.

Guidebook interpretation (PDF p. 227):
The organisation shall use clinical audits as a quality improvement tool to
improve the quality of patient care. The clinical audit could be
retrospective/prospective in nature. The topic for audit could be
disease-based, cost-based, community-based or based on morbidity (length of
stay). The organisation shall conduct one clinical audit per clinical
department once in two years as per the scope of services. The organisation
needs to take care to differentiate clinical audit from research projects.

Stop-work check: Commitment — no stop-work trigger. Note: "once in two years"
is verbatim in the source (shall) — preserve this exact frequency without
replacing it with a vaguer term. The clinical audit topics (disease-based,
cost-based, community-based, morbidity/LOS) are illustrative examples; the
organisation selects its own topics.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PSQ.5.b
Level: Commitment | No star

Verbatim OE text:
The parameters to be audited are defined by the organisation.

Guidebook interpretation (PDF p. 228):
As clinical audits are standards-based, they must be done using predefined
parameters so that there is no bias. The organisation shall lay down the
objectives, the standards against which the audit will be conducted, develop
a checklist where required, sampling and data collection guidelines and
preparation of the report. The audit shall encompass aspects of clinical and
nursing care.

Stop-work check: Commitment — no stop-work trigger. The predefined-parameters
requirement (objectives, standards, checklist, sampling/data-collection
guidelines, report) is mandatory (shall) — all components must be referenced
in any draft.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PSQ.5.c
Level: Achievement | No star

Verbatim OE text:
Medical and nursing staff participate in clinical audit.

Guidebook interpretation (PDF p. 228):
The organisation shall identify such personnel. It could be a mix of
clinicians, administrators and nurses. These could be members of the core
committee/quality assurance committee etc.

Stop-work check: Achievement — no stop-work trigger. The personnel
composition (clinicians, administrators, nurses) is illustrative; the
organisation identifies the specific mix. Quality assurance committee
membership is one acceptable mechanism, not the only one.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PSQ.5.d
Level: Commitment | No star

Verbatim OE text:
Patient and staff anonymity are maintained.

Guidebook interpretation (PDF p. 228):
This means that the names of the patients and the hospital staff who may
figure in the audit documents must not be disclosed nor any reference is made
to them in public discussions/conferences. This is at the stage of report
preparation and dissemination. The staff participating in the audit shall
maintain patient and staff anonymity and not reveal names.

Stop-work check: Commitment — no stop-work trigger. Anonymity applies at two
points: report preparation and dissemination — both are covered by the shall.
Any draft must reflect both stages, not just one.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PSQ.5.e
Level: Commitment | No star

Verbatim OE text:
Clinical audits are documented.

Guidebook interpretation (PDF p. 228):
The organisation could use a checklist with the predefined parameters, and the
audit findings could be recorded on this sheet. After the audit, a report shall
be prepared, highlighting the key findings of the audit.

Stop-work check: Commitment — no stop-work trigger. The post-audit report is
mandatory (shall); the checklist/recording mechanism is recommended (could).
Any draft must reflect the mandatory report while treating the checklist
format as one acceptable option.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PSQ.5.f
Level: Commitment | No star

Verbatim OE text:
Remedial measures are implemented.

Guidebook interpretation (PDF p. 228):
All remedial measures as ascertained shall be documented and implemented, and
improvements thereof recorded to complete the audit cycle. This shall
preferably be done based on the root-cause analysis.

Stop-work check: Commitment — no stop-work trigger. Three mandatory (shall)
actions: document, implement, and record improvements — all three close the
audit cycle. Root-cause analysis is preferred but not mandatory (shall
preferably). Any draft must reflect all three mandatory steps.
