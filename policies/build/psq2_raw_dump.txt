PSQ.2 — Raw Source Dump
=======================

Standard text (verbatim):
The organisation implements a structured quality improvement and continuous monitoring
programme.

OE count: 9 | COREs: a, i | Stars: a★, b★, e★, f★, h★, i★


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PSQ.2.a
Level: CORE | Star ★ (documentation required)

Verbatim OE text:
The quality improvement programme is developed, implemented and maintained by a
multi-disciplinary committee.*

Guidebook interpretation (PDF p. 219):
The quality improvement programme shall be developed, implemented and maintained in a
structured manner.

It shall be integrated across the organisation and provide a framework for risk
management, ongoing monitoring and performing improvements based on reviews.

The roles and responsibilities of the multidisciplinary committee are defined, and it
shall have representation from management, various clinical and support departments of
the organisation. This committee shall receive inputs on significant deliberations from
other committees in the organisation. The committee may be called as the core
committee, quality improvement committee etc.

Stop-work check: CORE — non-negotiable. Absence of a multidisciplinary committee
developing, implementing and maintaining the quality improvement programme is CORE
non-compliance.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PSQ.2.b
Level: Commitment | Star ★ (documentation required)

Verbatim OE text:
The quality improvement programme is comprehensive and covers all the major elements
related to quality assurance.*

Guidebook interpretation (PDF pp. 219–220):
The quality improvement programme shall be documented in the form of Quality
Improvement Manual. The quality improvement programme shall incorporate the goals and
objectives of the programme, framework for performing quality improvement activities
including data collection, important indicators as identified, frequency of mock drills,
audit schedules, committees and their terms of reference, review policy and
implementation of corrective and preventive action. Utilisation review preferably could
be part of quality improvement program. The quality assurance programme for specific
areas like Laboratory, Imaging, Emergency, Blood center, Operation theatre and the
Intensive Care Unit(s) is also summarised.

Refer to AAC.7.a., AAC.9.a., COP2.h., COP8.g., COP9.f., COP 14.i., COP 14.j. and
PSQ.2.d.

Stop-work check: Commitment — no stop-work trigger.

PSQ.2.c
Level: Excellence | No star

Verbatim OE text:
The quality improvement programme improves process efficiency and effectiveness.

Guidebook interpretation (PDF p. 220):
The quality improvement programme encourages the use of quality tools, novel strategies
to improve both clinical and managerial processes. The impact of the managerial process
innovations may be at the level of the department or organisation-wide. Innovations may
be targeted to improve patient safety, improve care delivery, to reduce costs, to
introduce environmentally-friendly measures etc. The management of the organisation
promotes these innovations.

Stop-work check: Excellence — no stop-work trigger.

PSQ.2.d
Level: Excellence | No star

Verbatim OE text:
The quality improvement programme focuses on appropriateness of clinical care.

Guidebook interpretation (PDF p. 220):
The doctors and nurses participate in utilization review which includes appropriateness
of length of the stay, procedures, investigations and treatment.

Stop-work check: Excellence — no stop-work trigger.

PSQ.2.e
Level: Commitment | Star ★ (documentation required)

Verbatim OE text:
There is a designated individual for coordinating and implementing the quality
improvement programme.*

Guidebook interpretation (PDF p. 220):
The designated individual shall undergo a structured training programme in the
implementation of accreditation standards (NABH Programme on Implementation of NABH
Standards) and with knowledge of statutory requirements, hospital quality improvement
principles and evaluation methodologies, hospital functioning and operations. For
example, accreditation coordinator, quality management representative, quality manager.

The designated individual shall report to the Top Management. The role and
responsibilities of the designated individual shall be defined and documented.

Also, champions in quality improvement are identified and developed across the
organisation and supported to drive improvement.

Stop-work check: Commitment — no stop-work trigger.

PSQ.2.f
Level: Commitment | Star ★ (documentation required)

Verbatim OE text:
The quality improvement programme identifies opportunities for improvement based on the
review at pre-defined intervals.*

Guidebook interpretation (PDF p. 221):
The quality improvement programme is a dynamic process. There is an outline of the
periodic review mechanisms at different levels, such as department/senior
administration/management reviews, etc. The Quality improvement programme needs to be
reviewed by the quality improvement committee at regular pre-defined intervals as
defined by the organisation in the quality improvement manual but at least once in
three months. The review shall include findings of audits, organisational performance,
analysis of key indicators as identified and determined by the organisation. The
minutes of the review meetings shall be recorded and maintained.

Stop-work check: Commitment — no stop-work trigger. Note: the "at least once in three
months" frequency is verbatim in the source and is mandatory (shall) — preserve it in
any draft without converting it to "regularly" or another vaguer term.

PSQ.2.g
Level: Commitment | No star

Verbatim OE text:
The quality improvement programme is reviewed and updated at least once a year.

Guidebook interpretation (PDF p. 221):
The update shall be done based on newer literature on quality improvement based on
audits, feedback mechanisms, the review carried out by the quality improvement
committee, etc.

In case the annual review does not identify any opportunities for improvement, the same
shall be documented in the minutes of the quality improvement committee meeting.

Stop-work check: Commitment — no stop-work trigger.

PSQ.2.h
Level: Commitment | Star ★ (documentation required)

Verbatim OE text:
Audits are conducted at regular intervals as a means of continuous monitoring.*

Guidebook interpretation (PDF pp. 221–222):
Choice and frequency of audits shall be defined for priority areas in the organisation
and for areas of concern as identified by trends in key performance indicators,
identified risk, etc. However, all the areas of the organisation shall be covered by a
hospital-wide internal audit at least once in 6 months as per a scheduled plan. This
internal audit shall be done by an identified staff or a multidisciplinary team trained
in NABH Accreditation standards for Hospitals. They shall assess areas independent of
their area of work. The internal audit of a particular area shall include all the
applicable standards and objective elements of NABH Accreditation standards for
Hospitals. At the end of the audit, there shall be a formal meeting to summarise the
findings and corrective and preventive measures shall be taken and documented.
Implementation of changes is verified and recorded.

Stop-work check: Commitment — no stop-work trigger. Note: "at least once in 6 months"
is verbatim in the source (shall) — preserve this exact frequency.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PSQ.2.i
Level: CORE | Star ★ (documentation required)

Verbatim OE text:
There is an established process in the organisation to monitor and improve the quality
of nursing care.*

Guidebook interpretation (PDF p. 222):
Monitoring shall be done through nursing audits. This could also be in the form of a
competency evaluation by written questionnaire or witnessed demonstration for key
nursing procedures such as medication administration, peripheral and intravenous cannula
placement, tracheostomy care etc. The organisation shall identify key performance
indicators that reflect excellence in nursing care. This shall be integrated with
quality improvement programme of the organisation.

Stop-work check: CORE — non-negotiable. Absence of an established process to monitor
and improve nursing care quality is CORE non-compliance. Note: the nursing procedure
examples (medication administration, cannula placement, tracheostomy care) are
illustrative ("could") — the organisation selects procedures appropriate to its scope.

NOTE ON "REFER TO APPENDED LIST" / NOTIFIABLE AND SENTINEL EVENTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CONFIRMED: The PSQ.2 source text (both the standards OCR and the Guidebook OCR)
contains NO "Refer to appended list" phrase and NO reference to notifiable or sentinel
event categories within any of its 9 OEs.

The user noted in a prior session that a ChatGPT draft for PSQ.2 included content
about reporting notifiable/sentinel events with what appeared to be an enumerated or
appended list of categories. That list was NOT sourced from NABH — it was ChatGPT
invention.

In the NABH 6th Edition source:
  — Sentinel events are defined in the Glossary (referenced at PSQ.1.c).
  — The requirement for the organisation to document its OWN list of sentinel events
    appears at PSQ.7.b ("The sentinel events relating to system or process deficiencies
    that are relevant and important to the organisation must be clearly defined. The list
    of the identified and relevant sentinel events shall be documented.").
  — PSQ.2 does not require a separate list of notifiable or sentinel events, and no
    such list is appended to or referenced within the PSQ.2 source text.

DRAFTING INSTRUCTION: Any PSQ.2 policy draft that mentions incident reporting,
notifiable events, or sentinel events must describe the obligation generically:
  — Example: "The organisation reports notifiable and sentinel events as defined in its
    own internal list" — do NOT enumerate specific event categories.
  — If the ChatGPT draft for PSQ.2 contains a bullet list of specific notifiable event
    types or sentinel event categories, those bullets should be removed or replaced with
    a generic reference to the organisation's internal definitions.
  — This note and the feedback rule apply across all PSQ drafts.
