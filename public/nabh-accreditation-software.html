Yes. AccredReady is built entirely on NABH 6th Edition standards, including all Objective Elements across all chapters. The platform is updated whenever NABH releases guidance changes.

Absolutely. AccredReady has a dedicated SHCO module covering SHCO Full Accreditation and SHCO Entry Level Certification, with a fee calculator for SHCO applications.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes. AccredReady is available on Android via Google Play Store (search "AccredReady") and works as a Progressive Web App on any device.
