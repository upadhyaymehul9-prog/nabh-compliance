Frequently Asked Questions

Is AccredReady based on NABH 6th Edition standards?

Yes. AccredReady is built entirely on NABH 6th Edition standards, including all 639 Objective Elements across 10 chapters. The platform is updated whenever NABH releases guidance changes.

Can I use AccredReady for SHCO accreditation?

Absolutely. AccredReady has a dedicated SHCO module covering SHCO Full Accreditation (408 OEs) and SHCO Entry Level Certification, with a fee calculator for SHCO applications.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes. AccredReady is available on Android via Google Play Store (search "AccredReady") and works as a Progressive Web App on any device.
