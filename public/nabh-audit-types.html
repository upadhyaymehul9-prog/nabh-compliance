Types of Audit in a
NABH Accredited Hospital

Audits are a key component of quality management and patient safety in NABH accredited hospitals. They help assess compliance, identify gaps, reduce risks, and drive continuous improvement in patient care and hospital operations.

Why Audits Are Non-Negotiable in NABH Hospitals

NABH does not just assess whether policies exist on paper — it evaluates whether your quality system is actively self-correcting. A hospital that conducts structured audits across all clinical and support functions demonstrates a culture of accountability. One that only audits when assessors arrive signals a reactive, compliance-only mindset.

The audit cycle — Plan, Conduct, Identify Gaps, Corrective Action, Re-Audit, Sustain Improvement — must be embedded in the hospital's routine operations, not treated as a pre-assessment exercise.

The 10 Major Types of Audits in NABH Hospitals

01

Clinical Audits

  • →Medical Record Audit — completeness, legibility, consent, discharge summaries
  • →Prescription Audit — dose, route, frequency, legibility, abbreviation compliance
  • →Antibiotic Stewardship Audit — appropriate use, de-escalation, culture correlation
  • →Surgical Case Audit — site marking, time-out, post-op notes, complication tracking
  • →Mortality & Morbidity Audit — review of adverse outcomes and contributing factors
  • →ICU Clinical Audit, Blood Transfusion Audit, Pain Assessment Audit, Consent Audit
02

Nursing Audits

  • →Nursing Documentation Audit — care plans, vitals charting, nursing notes completeness
  • →Medication Administration Audit — 5 rights compliance, documentation, wastage records
  • →IV Therapy Audit — insertion date, site condition, flushing documentation
  • →Handover Audit — SBAR use, verbal and written handover completeness
  • →Fall Prevention Audit, Pressure Injury Prevention Audit, Catheter Care Audit
  • →Patient Education Audit — discharge teaching documentation and comprehension
03

Patient Safety Audits

  • →IPSG Audit — all 6 International Patient Safety Goals compliance
  • →Patient Identification Audit — wristband use, two-identifier verification at every step
  • →Medication Safety Audit — high-alert meds, LASA segregation, sound-alike protocols
  • →Surgical Safety Checklist Audit — WHO checklist compliance in every OT procedure
  • →Time-Out Compliance Audit, Fall Risk Assessment Audit, Pressure Injury Prevention Audit
04

Infection Control Audits

  • →Hand Hygiene Audit — 5 moments compliance, technique observation, rub availability
  • →Biomedical Waste Audit — colour coding, bin fill level, labelling, transport records
  • →Injection Safety Audit — single-use compliance, sharps disposal, no recapping
  • →Central Line Bundle Audit, Ventilator Bundle Audit, Catheter Bundle Audit
  • →Environmental Cleaning Audit — terminal cleaning, high-touch surfaces, wet mopping
  • →Sterilisation Process Audit, CSSD Audit, PPE Compliance Audit
05

Quality Indicator Audits

  • →Nursing Quality Indicators — falls rate, pressure injury rate, medication error rate
  • →Medication Error Audit — near-miss tracking, error classification, CAPA closure
  • →Patient Satisfaction Audit — survey completion rates, score trends, action taken
  • →Readmission Audit — 30-day unplanned readmissions, root cause analysis
  • →Mortality Rate Analysis, Turnaround Time Audit, Incident Reporting Audit
06

Facility & Safety Audits

  • →Fire Safety Audit — extinguisher expiry, exit signage, unobstructed evacuation routes
  • →Emergency Preparedness Audit — mock drill records, code response teams, equipment
  • →Electrical Safety Audit — earthing certification, no exposed wiring, UPS functionality
  • →Medical Gas Audit — cylinder storage, manifold pressure, pipeline labelling
  • →Water Quality Audit, Lift Safety Audit, Radiation Safety Audit, Hazardous Material Audit
  • →Disaster Management Audit — disaster plan documentation and drills
07

Departmental Audits

  • →Laboratory: NABL Compliance Audit, Sample Collection Audit, Critical Value Reporting Audit
  • →Pharmacy: Drug Storage Audit — temperature logs, expiry checks, narcotics register
  • →Pharmacy: Expiry Audit, Controlled Drug Audit — dual-key access, daily reconciliation
  • →Radiology: Radiation Safety Audit, Equipment Calibration Audit
  • →Blood Bank: Blood Storage Audit, Transfusion Reaction Audit
08

HR & Training Audits

  • →Credentialing Audit — qualification certificates, registration, reference checks for all doctors
  • →Privileging Audit — clinical privilege forms signed and updated for every practitioner
  • →Staff Competency Audit — competency assessment records for all clinical staff
  • →Mandatory Training Compliance Audit — fire safety, hand hygiene, BLS, POSH training records
  • →Induction Training Audit, License Verification Audit — hepatitis B vaccination status
09

Support Services Audits

  • →Housekeeping Audit — cleaning schedules, chemical use, staff technique compliance
  • →Laundry Audit — linen segregation, washing temperature, turnaround time
  • →Dietary Audit — food safety, menu compliance, special diet accuracy, hygiene
  • →Security Audit — access control, visitor management, CCTV coverage
  • →Ambulance Audit — equipment checklist, vehicle hygiene, paramedic credentials
  • →Maintenance Audit — preventive maintenance schedules, breakdown response times
10

Statutory & Regulatory Audits

  • →Biomedical Waste Management Audit — BMW rules compliance, authorisation renewal
  • →Fire NOC Compliance Audit — valid fire NOC, compliance with NOC conditions
  • →PNDT Audit — PC-PNDT register maintenance, form F completion, machine registration
  • →Pharmacy Act Compliance Audit — licensed pharmacist on duty, dispensing records
  • →Sapur Law Compliance Audit, Pollution Control Compliance Audit

The Audit Cycle: How to Make Audits Systematic

Conducting audits once and filing the report is not what NABH expects. The audit cycle must be a living process that continuously improves the hospital's quality systems.

Step 1–2
Plan & Conduct
Identify areas and objectives. Collect data and assess compliance against NABH standards.
Step 3–4
Identify & Correct
Analyse findings and root causes. Implement corrective and preventive actions with closure evidence.
Step 5–6
Re-Audit & Sustain
Verify effectiveness of corrections. Monitor and drive excellence as a continuing practice.

Benefits of a Structured Audit Programme

  • ✓Improves patient safety and quality of care at every touchpoint
  • ✓Ensures continuous compliance with NABH standards between assessments
  • ✓Identifies risks and reduces errors before they become incidents
  • ✓Enhances operational efficiency across clinical and support departments
  • ✓Promotes a culture of accountability and excellence in daily work
  • ✓Supports data-driven decision making at the leadership level
