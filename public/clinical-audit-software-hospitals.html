Most hospitals know they're supposed to run clinical audits. Few have a clear, step-by-step way to actually do one. AccredReady's Clinical Audit module fixes that — a guided method, fully worked examples, and a tracker for your own hospital's audits, built into the same platform you use for NABH readiness.

Why "run a clinical audit" is easier said than done

Clinical audit is one of the most frequently cited gaps on NABH surveys — not because hospitals don't understand quality improvement, but because the method itself is unfamiliar. Picking a topic, writing a measurable standard, deciding a sample size, collecting data without bias, and closing the loop with a re-audit is a five-stage discipline most CQI teams have never been formally trained in.

Software that just gives you a blank form to log "audit findings" doesn't solve that. You need to actually learn the method first — then have somewhere to apply it.

What the AccredReady Clinical Audit module actually does

This is a standalone module inside AccredReady, available alongside your HCO, SHCO and ELC programmes once you sign in. It has three parts:

1. Learn — the method, explained from scratch

A free, no-prior-knowledge-required walkthrough covering what a clinical audit is, how to build your team, ethics and patient confidentiality, and then the five working stages: topic selection, standards & targets, sampling & data collection, results analysis, and making changes stick.

2. Examples — full worked audits, not just theory

Four complete, realistic audit write-ups (fall-risk assessment timing, antibiotic prophylaxis timing, consent form completeness, hand hygiene compliance) — each with the plain-English story of what's actually happening on the ward, and the formal report structure an assessor or your medical director would expect to see.

3. My Audits — track your hospital's real audits

Create an audit project, set your aim and Donabedian focus (structure/process/outcome), log your standard and target, move it through an eight-stage tracker from topic to re-audit, and keep a running record your CQI committee can actually present.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Clinical Audit module free?

The "Getting Ready" section — what a clinical audit is, building your team, ethics — is free for every signed-in user. The five working stages, the worked examples, and the audit tracker unlock with an active subscription.

No. The Learn section is written to need no prior knowledge — it explains the method from first principles before you ever open the tracker.
