NABH ELC state incentives — how SHAs apply the PMJAY uplift

The national rate is clear. Getting your State Health Agency to activate it — and keeping the tier live through ELC renewal — is where hospitals leave money on the table.

MU Dr. Mehul Upadhyay · Healthcare Operations Leader · Published: August 2026

Under AB-PMJAY, hospitals with valid NABH Entry Level Certification typically receive a 10% uplift on base package rates; Full Accreditation typically receives 15%. States do not invent a separate ELC bonus — they implement the national differential through their State Health Agency (SHA) under the state-converged scheme (for example KASP in Kerala or CMHIS in Nagaland). Your certificate alone does not change claim rates until the SHA updates your reimbursement tier. Source framework: AB-PMJAY operational guidelines; programme standards at nabh.co.

Decision check: If your hospital is PMJAY-empanelled and runs a steady claim volume, ELC is usually the faster path to the first 10% uplift. Use the 2-year ELC cycle to build toward Full Accreditation and the 15% tier. See the national ELC incentive guide for the fee-vs-claim maths.

National rates vs what your state actually pays

Think of the NHA guideline as the ceiling table. Your SHA publishes (or configures) the same differentials inside the state scheme schedule, then applies them after verifying accreditation documents.

Status Typical uplift Who activates it Validity watch
No accreditation Base rate — —
NABH Entry Level Certification +10% State Health Agency tier update ELC = 2 years
NABH Full Accreditation +15% State Health Agency tier update HCO/SHCO Full = 4 years

Exact compounding rules (for example metro or state-location add-ons) vary by SHA schedule. Never assume two incentives stack — read the state schedule or ask the SHA claims cell.

Documented state-scheme examples

These examples show how published SHA schedules mirror the national table. They are not a complete 28-state directory — treat them as proof that the incentive is real and SHA-mediated, then verify your own state portal.

Kerala — KASP / AB-PMJAY

Kerala converges AB-PMJAY with state schemes under Karunya Arogya Suraksha Padhathi (KASP), operated by the State Health Agency. Published SHA materials list Entry Level NABH / NQAS at 10% and Full NABH / JCI at 15% over base procedure rates. Kerala has also run quality-certification workshops with NHA and QCI for SHA and district coordinators. Source: sha.kerala.gov.in.

Nagaland — CMHIS / AB-PMJAY

Nagaland CMHIS contract schedules publish differential package pricing where Entry Level NABH / AB PM-JAY Silver is 10% and Full NABH / JCI / NQAS / Gold-equivalent pathways are 15%. The same schedules describe additional location-based incentives and note that NABH and NQAS incentives are not double-counted — hospitals eligible under both are incentivised under NQAS only. Source: Nagaland CMHIS RFP schedules (public tender documents).

Other states — same national table, different portal names

Most implementing states follow the NHA differential and rename the scheme locally. Your job is not to memorise every acronym — it is to (1) hold a valid NABH ELC or Full certificate, (2) keep PMJAY empanelment active, and (3) get the SHA tier updated before you expect enhanced remittances.

How to activate the incentive in any state

  1. 1

    Earn NABH Entry Level Certification

    Complete Entry Level Certification under the current 2nd Edition programme (unified for ≤50 and 51+ beds). ELC is a 2-year certification cycle. Confirm eligibility (functional ≥6 months; standards ≥3 months; occupancy thresholds) on nabh.co; see the ELC eligibility guide and ELC software page.

  2. 2

    Confirm PMJAY / state-scheme empanelment

    The uplift applies to empanelled hospitals under the state-converged AB-PMJAY scheme. Non-empanelled facilities with ELC do not receive PMJAY rate incentives.

  3. 3

    Submit the certificate to the SHA

    Upload the digital certificate on the SHA empanelment portal, or follow the district-office process your state still uses. Keep acknowledgement numbers.

  4. 4

    Reconcile the first enhanced settlements

    After the tier change (often 15–30 working days), compare settled amounts to base package rates. If claims still pay at base rate, escalate with the SHA acknowledgement and certificate copy.

  5. 5

    Protect the tier at renewal

    Start ELC renewal at least 3 months before expiry. A lapse resets you to base rate until the new certificate is issued and the SHA re-updates the tier. Track renewal on AccredReady alongside OE compliance.

AB-PMJAY Bronze, Silver, Gold — how it sits next to NABH ELC

The AB-PMJAY Quality Certification programme (NHA + QCI) offers Bronze, Silver, and Gold milestones. Public programme materials describe Silver as aligned with NABH Entry Level / NQAS (often 10%) and Gold with Full NABH / JCI (often 15%). Hospitals that already hold NABH ELC may use a simplified Silver path. Use this as a parallel quality ladder — not a substitute for reading your SHA schedule. Source: pmjay.qcin.org.

When ELC incentive ROI is worth the SHA effort

If your hospital processes even a modest monthly PMJAY volume, the 10% uplift usually recovers the ELC fee well inside the 2-year cycle. The operational catch is SHA activation lag and renewal gaps — both are process failures, not clinical ones. Quality managers who own the certificate-to-tier handoff protect more revenue than those who stop at “we got ELC.”

Frequently asked questions

Do all Indian states give the same NABH ELC incentive on PMJAY claims?

The national AB-PMJAY guideline sets Entry Level NABH at 10% and Full NABH at 15% over the base package rate. States implement this through their SHA under the state-converged scheme name. Activation steps and claim processing differ by state — confirm the current tier table with your SHA.

How does Kerala apply the NABH Entry Level incentive?

Under KASP / AB-PMJAY, published SHA materials list 10% for Entry Level NABH (or NQAS) and 15% for Full NABH or JCI. Source: sha.kerala.gov.in.

How does Nagaland treat NABH ELC under its health insurance scheme?

CMHIS schedules list Entry Level NABH / AB PM-JAY Silver at 10% and Full NABH / JCI / NQAS / Gold-equivalent pathways at 15%, with additional location rules that may apply. Source: Nagaland CMHIS RFP schedules.

Is AB-PMJAY Bronze / Silver / Gold the same as NABH ELC?

No. It is a separate NHA–QCI ladder. Silver is benchmarked to NABH Entry Level / NQAS; Gold aligns with Full NABH / JCI. Hospitals with NABH ELC often have a simplified Silver route — confirm with your SHA and pmjay.qcin.org.

What must I submit to my State Health Agency after getting NABH ELC?

Submit your valid ELC certificate via the SHA empanelment portal or district process. After the tier updates (often 15–30 working days), later claims attract the enhanced rate automatically.

Sources: AB-PMJAY quality incentive framework as published by State Health Agencies (including Kerala SHA / KASP materials), Nagaland CMHIS schedule documents, AB-PMJAY Quality Certification at pmjay.qcin.org, and NABH programme information at nabh.co. Always verify live rates with your SHA before financial planning.
