Why we built it

Patients travel 20–30 km only to find the doctor absent. Doctors wait in empty clinics. Both sides lose — and small clinics cannot afford enterprise hospital software.

BookMyClinic fixes the first mile: patients know the doctor is available before they visit. Doctors see tomorrow's schedule tonight.

It pairs naturally with OPD Manager (run the visit inside the clinic) and AccredReady (when the hospital is ready for NABH).
